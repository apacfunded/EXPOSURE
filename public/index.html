Rules

  • Call out on Exposure. Open a coin's page and tap Call out. Your wallet signs it, and you need at least 100,000 tokens (0.01% of supply).
  • One callout per coin per round. It stays in until that coin's next payout.
  • Holding. Counts the lower of what you held when you called and what you hold now. Buying more later doesn't add; selling lowers it, and selling out drops you to zero.
  • PnL. How much the coin's market cap rose since your call. Losses count as 0%, gains count up to +400%.
  • Score = √holding × (1 + PnL). Holding is capped at 1% of supply, so one whale can't take the whole pool.
  • Top 20 bonus. 5% of every payout on every coin builds the Top 20 pool. Once a day it pays the 20 callers with the biggest % gain on their calls in the last 7 days. #1 gets 20 shares, down to #20 with 1.
  • 0.5 SOL max per wallet per payout. Anything over stays in the pool for the next round.

    Nothing here is financial advice

    Exposure is a tool for launching coins and rewarding the people who promote them. Nothing on this site is investment, financial, legal or tax advice. Do your own research, and only spend what you can afford to lose.

    Memecoins are extremely risky

    Most coins lose most or all of their value, often within hours. Prices can move very fast, liquidity can disappear, and anyone can launch a coin. A coin being on Exposure doesn't mean we checked it, vouch for it, or know who made it.

    Payouts aren't guaranteed

    Payouts only happen when a coin earns enough creator fees for its pool to reach 1 SOL. A coin with little trading may never pay out. Your share depends on your holding and the coin's gain since your call compared with other callers, so estimates on this site can change until a payout is sent.

    We rely on other services

    Coins are created and traded on pump.fun, and prices come from pump.fun and DexScreener. If any of them change or go down, scoring and payouts may be delayed, paused or changed. Tokenized stocks used as pairs are issued by third parties, and may not be available where you live.

    Fair play

    Using many wallets to farm callouts, wash trading to fake gains, rating yourself, or otherwise gaming the system can get a wallet excluded from payouts, ranks and ratings. We may adjust the scoring rules to stop abuse. When we do, we'll announce it on X and update How it works.

    Your wallet, your responsibility

    Exposure never holds your coins and never asks for your seed phrase or private key. Connecting a wallet only shares your public address, and signing a callout or rating message can't move funds. We will never DM you first. Anyone who does is not us.

    How it works

    One loop, on repeat

    1 · Launch

    Coin goes live

    Anyone launches on pump.fun through Exposure. That coin's creator fees fill its own pool.

    2 · Call it

    Post a callout

    Holders open the coin on Exposure and tap Call out. Their wallet signs it, nothing is paid.

    3 · Hold and pump

    Calls are scored

    Your score is your holding (capped at 1% of supply) times how much the coin gained since your call.

    4 · Get paid

    Pool splits

    Every time a coin's pool reaches 1 SOL, 70% pays that round's callers by score, 5% goes to the Top 20 bonus (paid daily) and 25% goes to the $EXPO buyback reserve.

    What is the Top 20 bonus?

    5% of every payout across all coins builds a bonus pool for the top 20 callers on Exposure, and it pays out once a day. They're ranked by the biggest % gain from their calls in the last 7 days. It pays out on top of their normal earnings, weighted by rank: #1 gets 20 shares, #2 gets 19, down to #20 with 1 share.

    When do payouts happen?

    Every time a coin's pool reaches 1 SOL from creator fees. Busy coins pay out many times a day; quiet coins pay out when they get there. Each payout covers the callouts posted since the last one.

    How is my share worked out?

    Score = √holding × (1 + PnL). Holding is the lower of what you held when you called and what you hold now, as a % of supply, capped at 1%. PnL is how much the coin's market cap rose since your call: losses count as 0% and gains count up to +400%. Your share of the 70% is your score divided by everyone's score in that round, capped at 0.5 SOL per payout.

    Can I call out more than once?

    One callout per coin per round. It stays in until that coin's next payout, then you can call it again. You can call out as many different coins as you like.

    Do I need to connect a wallet?

    Yes, to call out. Your wallet signs the callout (a free message, nothing is sent) so we know it's really you and where to send your payout. You also connect to launch a coin, to rate callers, or to see everything on My Exposure. Connecting shares your public address only. It can't move funds.

    What's on my caller profile?

    Every caller gets a public profile with their rank, rating, win rate, best call, earnings, star rating from holders, open callouts and payouts. Click any name on the site to open it.

    Why holdings and PnL?

    Both cost real money to fake. To score, you have to actually hold the coin, and your call only earns more if the coin really goes up after it. Every buy that pushes a coin up helps every holder anyway.

    What's the whale cap?

    Any bag above 1% of supply counts the same as 1%, and holdings are square-rooted, so a bag 4× bigger scores 2× more. One big holder can't take the whole payout.

    Can I call out on pump.fun or X too?

    Post wherever you like, and use the share link to send people to the coin. Only callouts made on Exposure count toward payouts, because they're signed by your wallet.

    How do caller stars work?

    Anyone holding an Exposure coin can rate a ranked caller from 1 to 5 stars. Each wallet gets one rating per caller and can change it later. You prove the wallet is yours by signing a free message, so nothing is sent. Stars show once a caller has at least 3 ratings. They're separate from a caller's rank, which comes only from how their calls actually did.

    Can I pair my coin with a stock?

    Yes. pump.fun's Custom Pairs let a coin trade against tokenized stocks like Nvidia or Tesla, against BTC or ETH, or against SOL and USDC. Pick the pair when you launch. Creator fees are collected in that asset, so a coin paired with Nvidia fills its pool with tokenized Nvidia and pays its callers in it. Custom pairs also let you set the creator fee anywhere from 0.05% to 1%.

    How does a stock-paired pool reach 1 SOL?

    The pool pays out when it's worth 1 SOL at the current price, whatever asset it holds. Callers and the Top 20 are paid in the paired asset. The 25% reserve share is swapped to SOL for the $EXPO buyback.

    When does $EXPO launch?

    After the launchpad. Until then, 25% of every payout builds the buyback reserve. On launch day the reserve buys $EXPO and burns it, and every payout after that burns too.
