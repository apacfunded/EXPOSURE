    How it works

    Four steps, on repeat

    The item's original cost goes back into the treasury to buy the next one.

    1. 1
      Trade $BLOXWHEEL

      Every buy and sell on pump.fun pays creator fees to the treasury.

    2. 2
      Fees buy Limiteds

      The treasury buys items with a Rolimons value and steady weekly sales.

    3. 3
      Hold to the target

      Nothing sells until it's up by the target, which is set above the ~43% break-even.

    4. 4
      Sell, buy back, burn

      The profit buys $BLOXWHEEL and burns it. Each burn is posted with its Solscan link.

    FAQ

    Questions

    Where do the fees come from?

    pump.fun pays the coin creator a share of trading volume. All of it goes to the public treasury wallet.

    Which items does the treasury buy?

    Only Limiteds with a Rolimons value and steady weekly sales. No projected items, never above value, and no more than 20% of the treasury in one item. Every buy is posted with its cost.

    Why is the target +50%?

    Roblox keeps about 30% of every resale. An item bought at 100 and sold at 150 nets about 105, so +50% leaves a small profit to burn. Anything under about +43% loses money.

    What if an item never hits the target?

    It stays in inventory. After 90 days it may be sold at break-even after fees to free up funds. Items are never sold at a loss.

    How do I verify burns?

    Every burn in the log links to its Solscan transaction. A weekly recap on X covers fees in, items held and total burned.

    Read before you buy

    Roblox items can lose value, and Roblox's rules restrict trading items for real money, so the account holding the inventory could be restricted. This is a memecoin, not an investment, and nothing here promises returns. Only buy what you can afford to lose.
